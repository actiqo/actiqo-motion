## 0 · The visual system in one paragraph

The afternoon is a **floor**:
- **Time runs left → right.** Hour lines recede into depth.
- **People run front → back.** Each has a lane: Mom's path, Leo's lane, Maya's lane.
- **Every logistic is planted at its real time on its real lane:** a small ground tick, a hairline stem and restrained Inter type. Nothing floats decoratively.

The only literal thread is the **orange dashed "Pickup · 6:30 · ?"** tied to Mom's hand. At the reveal, the far floor **folds up into a wall**. The same hour lines continue unbroken across the fold, and the wall is the real **Game Plan**. ACTIQO was the structure under the afternoon all along; a change of viewpoint reveals it.

**Palette.** Production tokens only:
- ivory `#FCFAF7`, navy `#0B1F3B`;
- green `#0F7A4B` / `#DCEFE3` (leave-by, resolved);
- orange `#C2410C` / `#FBEAD9` (the unassigned pickup **only**);
- blue `#1D4ED8` / `#E6EEFE` (what to bring);
- child identity from production `CHILD_COLORS`: Leo `#0EA5E9`, Maya `#7C3AED`.

**Type.** Inter for everything operational. Fraunces only for the "Game Plan" title, the leave-by band and the end card.

## 4 · Character choreography

**Mom.** Editorial, natural proportions (about 7.5 heads).
- **Clothes:** an oat trench (open), ACTIQO navy knit (her one brand-colour garment), washed denim and white sneakers.
- **Hair:** a dark low bun.
- **Face:** the face is reduced to an almond eye and a soft brow line.
- **Emotion through posture only.**

**One "load" parameter (0→1→0) drives:** shoulder height (shrug), forward lean (5° → 10° → 2°), head angle, stride length and arm swing. These are the poses used in the styleframes:

| Pose | Time | Body | Hands |
|---|---|---|---|
| reading | 0–3 s | 5° lean, head down 14° | phone at chest; bag on shoulder |
| glancing | 3–9 s | 7° lean, head lifting to the world | phone at hip; cleats in far hand |
| weight | 9–12 s | stopped, 10° lean, shoulders up, head down | both hands occupied |
| steady | 12–17 s | 4° lean, head level, walking toward the wall | phone + cleats |
| (release) | 17–23 s | two discrete shoulder drops, at 18.4 and 20.0 | cleats → What to bring; bag → Dad |
| free | 26–30 s | 2° lean, longest stride, arms swinging | **free** |

**Walk cycle:**
- loaded: a 14-frame half cycle with short steps;
- calm: a 16-frame half cycle, with stride +18% and arm swing +40%.

**Contacts:** feet plant exactly on lane lines. She never slides on the grid.

**Dad:** enters at 18.4 s, takes the bag in one overlapping action (reach, lift, shoulder) and exits in 1.0 s.

**Grandma:**
- **Look:** silver short hair, stone-blue cardigan coat.
- **Walk:** a slower 18-frame cycle.
- **Action:** she enters at 24.4 s and catches the thread with an open hand. It's the only gesture-led "catch" in the film.

## 7 · Transition choreography

- **Message → world (5.2 s):** the words "Field 7" lift out of the bubble and travel along a shallow arc, about 18 f on the enter curve, into Leo's marker sub-line. The bubble's remaining text fades.
- **Question → thread (7.4 s):** "Who has pickup?" contracts horizontally into a point at the phone. From that point the orange dashed thread spools out toward 6:30. *The question literally becomes the loose end she carries.*
- **Alignment (12.0 s):** every tick and line resolves to exact grid intersections, a 4 px max correction over 6 f. It is subtle but it primes the eye for structure.
- **The fold (12.6–14.4 s)** is the signature move:
  - The floor beyond Mom's path rotates about the fold line.
  - Hour lines are continuous across the fold: on the wall they become the vertical construction lines the cards sit on.
  - Lanes become row dividers.
  - Each marker keeps its X (time position) while folding, then slides at most 40 px to its row, so the eye can follow it.
  - Green, sky and blue keep their hue into their real components, and the orange stays orange.
  - Nothing disappears: ghost labels hold on the fold line for 24 f.
- **Put down (17–20 s):** dashed registration lines draw from the hand to the row (12 f). The object travels along the line (14 f) and lands as the row's content. The hand opens; there is no "poof".
- **Thread transfer (24.4–26 s):** the thread's origin slides from Mom's hand to Grandma's hand (18 f). On the resolve frame, orange dashed becomes a neutral solid line (6 f), matching the row's state change, then fades (18 f).
- **To end card (27.6 s):** the world's lines fade to the ivory ground (24 f) and the wordmark fades in (18 f). The logo is never animated internally.

**Motion rules** (ACTIQO motion system):
- entrances use `cubic-bezier(0.2,0.8,0.2,1)`;
- structural moves use `cubic-bezier(0.2,0,0,1)`;
- the camera uses `cubic-bezier(0.65,0,0.35,1)`;
- no bounce, glow, particles, portal or phone fly-in.

## 8 · Sound and music direction

- **Music:** an intimate, warm score at about 86 BPM.
  - 0–3 s: solo felt piano, sparse.
  - 3–9 s: a soft muted-string pulse enters and density rises gently with each marker. Each marker gets a soft tuned pluck in the same key, so the load builds *musically*.
  - 9–12 s: the pulse stops and one sustained low chord rings out under silence. It isn't ominous, just full.
  - **12.6–14.4 s, the fold:** a single rising string swell that **resolves exactly as the wall reaches its final angle**, into a major chord. This is the film's musical "click".
  - 17–23 s: the piano returns with a lighter, half-time feel. Each put-down lands on a beat with a soft wooden settle.
  - **25.4 s, Grandma's resolve:** a warm two-note horn or cello figure, the emotional peak.
  - 26–30 s: an open, unresolved-then-resolved cadence. The end card sits on a held tonic.
- **SFX:**
  - phone buzz, at 0.4 s only;
  - footsteps on a soft paper floor;
  - a cleats lace rustle and a bag strap creak at the handoffs;
  - a soft "paper settling" at the fold.
  - **No UI beeps or whooshes.**
- **Mix:** the music sits under the SFX through 0–12 s and over them from 12 s. The last 24 frames carry only the tonic.
