*Creative development only: no code, no animation, no character assets. Film 01 (S1–S5 locked at v02-1) is untouched.*

**Character baseline, shared by all three.** Editorial illustration: 7½-head proportions and a strong readable silhouette at thumbnail size.
- **Face:** eyes as two soft strokes, a hint of brow, no mouth detail beyond a line.
- **Clothes:** muted real-world tones (oat, stone, washed denim) with ACTIQO navy in **one** garment.
- **Brand colour:** teal appears **only** on ACTIQO surfaces, never on the person.
- **Rendering:** flat colour with one soft form shadow and a subtle paper grain. No outlines, or a very light navy hairline.
- **Emotion:** carried by posture: shoulder height, head angle, stride length and arm swing.
- **Avoid:** stick figures, mascots, big-eyed cartoon faces, 3D-render gloss and "corporate Memphis" limbs.

# TREATMENT A · "THE PATH"
### The week is a road. ACTIQO lays it out ahead of you.

## 1 · Creative idea
The week is a literal path the parent walks, left to right.
- **Time is the ground.** Each day is a stretch of pavement and the hours are faint markings.
- **Events are landmarks** at the side of the road: goalposts for soccer, a barre for dance.
- **Every loose logistic is a thing on the path:** a leave time sprayed on the ground, a pair of cleats, a sign saying Field 4 with a sticker "7" over it, Maya's dance studio arriving in the parent's lane at the same moment as the field.

The parent keeps stopping, looking, remembering. Then ACTIQO **resurfaces the road itself**: the path becomes the Home Next Up card they walk onto, then Game Plan, then Assignments. The logistics snap into their rows like paving stones settling into a clean line. The last shot is the whole week ahead as a calm, lit road, with Grandma walking in from a side path to meet them.

## 2 · Character art direction
- **Build:** full-body side view, the most legible silhouette for a walk.
- **Wardrobe:** oat trench, navy knit, rolled denim, white sneakers.
- **Props:** a tote on one shoulder (the load), and a phone in one hand, only in the final act.
- **Start:** shoulders slightly high and forward, short quick steps, head turning often (scanning).
- **End:** longer stride, shoulders down, head level, free arm swinging.
- **Proportions stay fixed.** Only posture changes, so the change reads as relief, not a new character.

## 3 · Environment concept
- **Ground plane:** a long horizontal ribbon in ivory `#FCFAF7` with navy hairline hour ticks, the Film 01 time grid laid flat.
- **Landmarks:** soft-shaded flat forms in a warm paper palette.
- **Layers:** foreground (grass edge, a cone), midground (path and landmarks), background (a faint skyline of house silhouettes = the village).
- **Semantic colour appears only when information matters:** green for leave, orange for the open pickup, child tints for Leo (sky) and Maya (violet).
- **ACTIQO surfaces:** white cards that rise out of the ground plane in 2.5D, with the real radii and shadows.

| 9–11 s | a held breath |
| 11–18 s | relief as items find a home |
| 18–22 s | warmth: someone else steps in |
| 22–30 s | confidence and lightness |

## 7 · Product features shown
Home Next Up · Leave by · Game Plan (What to bring, Who's doing what, location) · Assignments (Assign → caregiver) · Ready Check · calendar sync (end card only).

## 8 · Where real UI appears
| Time | Real UI |
|---|---|
| 11.0 s | Next Up hero, as floor. |
| 14.5 s | Game Plan, as stepping stones. |
| 19.0 s | Assignments row change. |
| 22.0 s | Ready Check, as a wall. |

Real UI is always rebuilt from the source at true proportions and copy, set in perspective. It is never a screenshot inside a phone.

## 9 · Character choreography
- **Walk cycles:**
  - "loaded": 12-frame half cycle, quick;
  - "neutral": 14 frames;
  - "light": 16 frames, longer stride, more arm swing.
- **Key poses:** a glance-back at the cleats (4 s), a hand to the temple at the overlap (7.5 s), the phone raise (11 s), a nod to Grandma (21 s), the head lifting at the crane (26 s).

## 10 · Camera choreography
Side-scrolling truck at the parent's walking speed. On key beats:
- a slow dolly-in at the overlap (6–9 s);
- a 12° downward tilt so the UI "floor" reads (11–18 s);
- the only crane move, at 25.5 s.

The camera never cuts; one continuous take.

## 11 · Transition language
- **The ground is the interface:** things are placed, not faded.
- **Road → UI:** the path texture resolves into the card surface, and hour ticks become hairline dividers.
- **Objects become rows:** they drop into rows along short arcs and settle on the ACTIQO enter curve. No spin, no bounce.

## 12 · On-screen copy
1. *"The activity lasts an hour."*
2. *"The coordination lasts all week."*
3. UI copy only through the middle.
4. End card: **"Everyone knows the plan."** · *Syncs with Google Calendar and Apple Calendar.*

## 13 · Sound and music
- **Music:** a warm felt piano and soft brushed pulse at about 92 BPM. It starts sparse, and layers add as the path fills.
- **Overlap (6–9 s):** a low sustained chord.
- **9–11 s:** silence apart from footsteps.
- **ACTIQO beat (11 s):** a clean major resolve, with the tempo feel moving to half-time for calm.
- **SFX:**
  - footsteps on paper;
  - soft wooden "clicks" as items settle into rows;
  - one bright two-note chime when Grandma's pickup resolves;
  - no UI beeps.
- **Voice:** none. Or a single quiet VO line at the end, to be decided.

## 14 · What makes it visually premium
One unbroken camera, true 2.5D depth, and editorial restraint. The only saturated colours are semantic. Real UI is set in physical perspective with correct shadows, and pacing slows as clarity arrives.

## 15 · What makes it uniquely ACTIQO
Leave-by as a *place on your route*. Pickup as an *open gap you can see*. Game Plan rows as the steps you actually take. The village literally walking in.

## 16 · Assets required
- **Character:** parent turnaround, side-view rig, 3 walk cycles, 6 acting poses and a phone-hold arm.
- **Grandma:** side view, walk and nod.
- **Landmarks:** soccer goal, dance studio door, bench, field sign, houses skyline.
- **Other:** path texture, paper grain, cleats and shin guards illustrations.
- **Music:** licensed or composed track.
- **SFX:** foley pack.

## 17 · What HyperFrames can produce directly
Real UI rebuilds (Home hero, Game Plan, Assignments, Ready Check), the path and hour grid, the 2.5D camera (a CSS 3D / GSAP transform stack), UI-to-floor perspective, row settling, threads, supers and the end card, plus audio mix and render.

## 18 · Separate illustrated/vector assets needed
- Parent and Grandma: rigged in After Effects (Duik) or Rive, exported to Lottie JSON or PNG sequences.
- Landmark and prop illustrations as SVG.
- Paper grain texture.

## 19 · 16:9 ↔ 9:16
- **16:9:** native, horizontal walking suits it.
- **9:16:**
  - the camera frames closer (knees up), and the path runs slightly diagonally, lower-left to mid-right;
  - UI "floor" moments tilt more steeply so cards stand up the frame;
  - the crane becomes a vertical rise.
- **Risk:** side-scroll loses about 40% of its sense of journey in 9:16.

# TREATMENT B · "WHAT YOU CARRY"
### Mental load as physical weight. ACTIQO takes it off your hands, one thing at a time.

## 1 · Creative idea
The parent walks toward camera through a softly abstract day, and every logistic **attaches to them**:
- a sticky note to the sleeve reading "Leave 4:48";
- a pair of cleats hooked on a finger;
- a map pin stuck to the tote (Field 7);
- a tiny clock on each wrist: Leo 5:30, Maya 5:15;
- a string tied to their pocket reading "Pickup · 6:30 · ?".

By 9 seconds they are *carrying the week*. It isn't a mess: the parent is capable, just loaded.

ACTIQO appears as clean white surfaces **beside** them. One by one, the parent *hands* each object to the surface, and it becomes its real row. Every hand-off lightens the body. The last object, the pickup string, isn't handed to the app. **It's handed to Grandma, through the app.**

## 2 · Character art direction
- **View:** three-quarter front, the most emotionally readable, since it shows the face.
- **Build:** medium-close to waist-up for most of the film, full body for the first and last shots.
- **Face:** slightly more facial nuance than A: brow and eyelids carry strain and relief.
- **Wardrobe:** stone overshirt with a navy tee; a canvas tote.
- **The objects:** drawn as crisp editorial still-lifes, slightly more detailed than the character, so the eye reads them as "real things".
- **Posture:** starts with a slight forward lean and one shoulder dropped under the tote. Ends upright, both hands free, and able to wave.

## 3 · Environment concept
- **Background:** a soft abstract "day" rather than a place. The ivory canvas has large, slow tonal fields: afternoon warm → evening cool.
- **Hints of place:** the Film 01 time grid appears faintly *behind* the parent as they walk, like weather.
- **ACTIQO surfaces:** they enter as tall white planes to the right of the parent at the same scale as the body, so the UI feels like furniture in their world.
- **Colour:** the only semantic colour is on the objects (green note, orange string) and on the UI.

## 4–5 · Storyboard with timestamps (30.0 s)

| Time | Shot | Action |
|---|---|---|
| 0.0–2.0 | **Walk-in** | Full body, walking toward camera. Hands free. Super: *"The activity lasts an hour."* |
| 2.0–8.0 | **Accumulation** | Medium, tracking back. Objects fly in and attach, one every 0.8 s, each with a tiny sound: "Leave 4:48" note to the sleeve · cleats on a finger · Field 7 pin on the tote ("Usually Field 4" crossed out on its tag) · two wrist clocks, Leo 5:30 and Maya 5:15 · the pickup string, "Pickup · 6:30 · ?". Shoulders drop by degrees. |
| 8.0–10.5 | **The weight** | Push-in to a close-up. The parent pauses mid-step and glances down at the full hands. Super: *"The coordination lasts all week."* Hold. |
| 10.5–13.0 | **ACTIQO arrives** | A white surface slides up beside them: the **Next Up hero**, real, at body scale, with Leave by / Game Plan / Check-In. The parent *looks* at it. The first sign of relief is a shoulder easing. |
| 13.0–19.0 | **Handing it over** | Medium two-shot, parent and surface. Hand-offs every 1.2 s: <br>• the note goes to the surface and becomes the green **Leave by 4:48 PM** band; <br>• the cleats go into **What to bring · Cleats, Shin guards**; <br>• the pin becomes the location line **Field 7**; <br>• the clocks become the session line **Thu 5:30 PM**, and Maya's dance is noted but, truthfully, stays on its own card behind. <br>The surface has become **Game Plan**. Each hand-off lifts posture a notch. |
| 19.0–23.5 | **The last thing** | Only the orange pickup string remains, on the finger. The surface shows **Assignments: No one on pickup · Assign**. The parent taps Assign. The string runs off-frame and is *caught* by Grandma, who walks into the two-shot holding it; it turns green. The UI reads **Grandma pickup · At 6:30 PM**. |
| 23.5–27.0 | **Free hands** | Wide. The parent walks on, both hands empty, and waves back at Grandma. Behind them the Game Plan surface recedes, calm. Dad passes the other way with the car keys, and **Dad drop-off** glows briefly. |
| 27.0–30.0 | **End card** | The parent exits frame, light. Wordmark · **"Everyone knows the plan."** · *Syncs with Google Calendar and Apple Calendar.* |

## 6 · Emotional progression
| Time | Feeling |
|---|---|
| 0–2 s | ease |
| 2–8 s | building load (recognition through the body) |
| 8–10.5 s | weight |
| 10.5–19 s | incremental relief, one release per beat |
| 19–23.5 s | trust: handing a real responsibility to someone |
| 23.5–30 s | freedom |

## 7 · Product features shown
Next Up hero · Leave by · Game Plan (What to bring, location, Who's doing what) · Assignments + caregiver assignment · calendar sync (end card).

## 8 · Where real UI appears
| Time | Real UI |
|---|---|
| 10.5 s | Next Up hero, standing plane. |
| 13.0–19.0 s | Transforms into Game Plan. |
| 19.0–23.5 s | Assignments. |

All standing surfaces sit at human scale beside the character.

## 9 · Character choreography
- **Walk:** one continuous walk toward camera, with the cycle slowing then lengthening.
- **Acting:**
  - micro-flinches as objects attach;
  - the "full hands" look-down;
  - an outstretched hand for each hand-off (overlapping action, shoulders following);
  - the wave;
  - a final unhurried stride.
- **Posture curve:** a single animated "load" parameter from 0 to 1 and back to 0 drives shoulder height, lean and step length. It is easy to rig and keeps consistency.

## 10 · Camera choreography
- **Tracking:** a slow backward track, so the parent stays mid-frame.
- **Push-in:** 8–10.5 s.
- **Framing:** widens to a two-shot when the surface arrives; the widest frame is at 23.5 s.
- **Lens:** gentle handheld drift, 1–2 px of noise at most, for humanity, turned off in the last 3 s for stillness.

## 11 · Transition language
- **Hand-off as transform:** each object *becomes* its row as it crosses from hand to surface. Shape and colour carry through: the green note → green band; the orange string → orange row → green once assigned.
- **The pickup string is the only thread in the film**, so its resolution is the climax.

## 12 · On-screen copy
Same two supers and end card as A. UI copy verbatim.

## 13 · Sound and music
- **Music:** intimate: a solo nylon guitar or soft Rhodes with a slow pulse.
- **Accumulation:** each object attaches with a distinct tactile SFX (paper, laces, pin, tick, string), building a little rhythm.
- **8–10 s:** the rhythm drops out, leaving a breath.
- **Hand-offs:** each is the *reverse* of its attach sound, softened, plus a gentle harmonic lift.
- **Grandma's catch:** a warm chord.
- **Ending:** open, airy.

## 14 · What makes it visually premium
Close, emotionally legible character acting (rare in product films), still-life-quality object illustration, and real UI at human scale. A single idea executed with precision.

## 15 · What makes it uniquely ACTIQO
ACTIQO is shown as the place you *put things down*, which is the true mental-load promise. The climax is a *shared responsibility*, not a notification, and it uses the real caregiver assignment.

## 16 · Assets required
- **Parent:** three-quarter-front rig with 3 face states (strained, neutral, easy) and 2 hand poses per hand (grip, open).
- **Grandma:** three-quarter view, walk-in and catch.
- **Dad:** a pass-by.
- **Objects (7):** note, cleats, shin guards, pin, two wrist clocks, pickup string. Each needs an "attached" and a "flying" state.
- **Other:** tonal backgrounds, music and SFX.

## 17 · What HyperFrames can produce directly
Real UI surfaces and their transforms, object→row morphs (SVG and DOM), the tonal background, the time-grid "weather", camera track and push, supers and end card, and the audio mix.

## 18 · Separate illustrated/vector assets needed
- Front-facing character acting is the hardest part of the three treatments. It needs a skilled 2D animator, working frame-by-frame or with an AE rig plus hand-drawn overlays.
- Delivered as Lottie or ProRes 4444 with alpha, composited in HyperFrames.

## 19 · 16:9 ↔ 9:16
- **9:16 is native.** A single figure walking toward camera, with the UI surface stacking *above/below* instead of beside. It is the best fit for Reels/TikTok.
- **16:9:** a side-by-side two-shot, character left and UI right, which is natural.
- **Risk:** the lowest of the three.

# TREATMENT C · "THE NEIGHBORHOOD"
### The week is a map of your town. ACTIQO is what it looks like from above.

## 1 · Creative idea
- **The town:** a warm isometric neighbourhood built out of the week. Streets are time, houses are family members, the field and the studio are destinations.
- **The walk:** the parent walks the streets while routes draw behind them as they think them through: home → field (drop-off), field → home, studio → home... with lines doubling back and crossing. Maya's route and Leo's route collide at a junction at 6:15–6:30.
- **The rise:** the camera rises. At a precise moment the isometric town flattens into a top-down plane, and that plane **is ACTIQO's UI**. Streets become card dividers, the field becomes the Game Plan card, and the routes become Who's doing what.
- **The village:** from above you can see Grandma's house two streets away, so the solution was always in the neighbourhood.

## 2 · Character art direction
- **Scale:** small in frame, 8–12% of the frame height, for most of the film, so silhouette and walk carry everything.
- **Look:** navy coat, a warm scarf, a slightly exaggerated readable stride.
- **Push-ins:** two close-ups for emotion (6 s, 24 s) in the same style at a larger scale.
- **Others:** Grandma, Dad and the kids appear as small figures at their houses and destinations.

## 3 · Environment concept
- **The town:** isometric at 30° from paper-cut layers: ivory ground, stone and oat buildings, navy roof accents.
- **Destinations:** the soccer field in muted green, the studio in muted violet (the child tints).
- **Paths:**
  - routes are navy hairlines;
  - the open pickup route is the orange dashed line from Film 01;
  - assigned routes turn green-inked.
- **At the flatten:** the town's grid aligns exactly to the ACTIQO card grid (16 pt spacing and radius at scale). It is a designed, mathematical match.

## 6 · Emotional progression
| Time | Feeling |
|---|---|
| 0–3 s | calm |
| 3–8.5 s | busyness, drawn as lines |
| 8.5–11 s | recognition: the knot |
| 11–15 s | awe and relief: the big reveal of the rise |
| 15–23 s | understanding, then warmth: the village |

## 7 · Product features shown
Home Next Up · Leave by · Game Plan (session card, What to bring, Who's doing what) · Assignments + caregiver · (optional 1 s) the week view · calendar sync (end card).

## 8 · Where real UI appears
The whole 11–23 s act is real UI, top-down and flat, full frame. It is the most "product-forward" of the three and the easiest to keep 100% truthful, because the UI is shown straight-on.

## 9 · Character choreography
- **Walk:** a readable small-scale cycle, 14 frames, with a turn at corners.
- **Close-ups:**
  - 9 s: a hand on the back of the neck, eyes tracing the routes;
  - 24 s: hands in pockets, easy stride, a small smile line.
- **Grandma:** a small figure who exits her house on assignment. The only time we see her closely is the final wide.

## 10 · Camera choreography
- **Iso follow:** 0–11 s.
- **One signature move:** 11–15 s, a crane plus 60° rotation to top-down. Ease in-out over 4 s: slow, confident, no whip.
- **UI act:** top-down glide.
- **Reverse move:** 23–27 s.
- **The flatten:** tuned so the town grid and the UI grid coincide *exactly* at 15.0 s. That frame is the hero still.

## 11 · Transition language
- **Grid registration:** the world and the interface share one grid, so the change is a *change of viewpoint*, not a cut.
- **Routes become responsibility:** the lines on the map are the rows in Who's doing what.

## 12 · On-screen copy
Same two supers and end card. Map labels use ACTIQO product words where possible.

## 13 · Sound and music
- **Music:** light mallets and marimba with pizzicato strings, like a small town waking, around 100 BPM.
- **The knot:** the rhythm gets polyrhythmic and slightly crowded.
- **The rise:** a long swelling strings chord that resolves exactly on the flatten (15.0 s).
- **UI act:** sparse.
- **Grandma's route:** a warm horn line.
- **Return:** the opening motif, now in a relaxed meter.
- **SFX:** distant car, birds, footstep ticks on the streets. At the flatten, a single soft "paper settling" sound.

## 14 · What makes it visually premium
The single signature camera move with the exact grid registration. A crafted miniature world. A real product shown full-bleed and straight-on, and a strong hero frame.

## 15 · What makes it uniquely ACTIQO
Coordination as *geography*: who drives where and when, which is literally what ACTIQO resolves. The village is physically on the map. The product is "the view from above" of your family's week.

## 16 · Assets required
- **Town:** isometric kit (houses ×6, field, studio, school, streets, car, trees) in paper-cut layers with front and top-down variants for the flatten.
- **Characters:** a small parent with walk and turn, 2 close-up acting shots, Grandma, Dad in the car, two small kids.
- **Other:** route system, music and SFX.

## 17 · What HyperFrames can produce directly
- Real UI (Home hero, Game Plan, Assignments).
- The route system (SVG path draws).
- Labels, supers and end card.
- The flatten, if the town is built from layered flat assets in CSS 3D. That means iso via `rotateX`/`rotateZ` on a flat plane, which makes the grid registration *mathematically exact*.
- The whole camera, and the audio.

## 18 · Separate illustrated/vector assets needed
- Town illustrations as layered SVG, each building with a roof and walls for the 2.5D extrusion.
- Characters as Lottie.
- A full 3D town (Blender) is **not** recommended: it costs more and reads less "ACTIQO".

## 19 · 16:9 ↔ 9:16
- **Both work.** The town is designed on a square tile.
- **16:9:** wider streets, and the camera leads the walk horizontally.
- **9:16:** streets run vertically, and the rise is a natural upward move. At the flatten the UI fills the vertical frame the way it does on a phone. That is strong.
- **Risk:** medium. The iso town is the largest asset build.
